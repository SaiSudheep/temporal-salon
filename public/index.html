Matching clients are offered the appointment in waitlist order. Each gets up to 15 minutes. A decline or timeout moves things along automatically.

Welcome them in

When a client accepts, the opening is filled here. Update Square, adjust their previous booking if needed, and mark the calendar update complete.

A few thoughtful details

A client can hold only one offer at a time. Old links cannot take an appointment from someone else. You can withdraw one offer and move on, or cancel the entire opening.

Outreach ends at the appointment start time or when no eligible requests remain. Clients who decline or time out stay on the waitlist for other openings.

This local prototype uses simulated texts and fictional demo clients. No messages are sent and no changes are made in Square. The 20-second demo option is clearly marked; normal offers last 15 minutes. All appointment times are Pacific time.
